About Me

Who I Am

I'm Manan Mishra, a Class XI PCM student at Sunbeam School Mughalsarai with a deep passion for science, technology, and innovation. From interning at IIT BHU's Department of Chemical Engineering to building AI-powered autonomous bots that tackle ocean pollution, I'm driven by the belief that young minds can solve the world's most pressing problems.

My journey spans research in nuclear engineering, hands-on robotics, data science, artificial intelligence, and entrepreneurship. I've competed and won at prestigious platforms including PRAGYAN at IIT BHU, Techradiance at IIT Delhi, and collaborated with NASA's Asteroid Search Campaign. Whether it's coding late into the night, building prototypes, or presenting innovations on national stages — I thrive at the intersection of curiosity and impact.

🌊 AI · Robotics · Environment

SWAMN

Ocean Sight — Blue Intelligence

An autonomous, AI-powered system designed to detect and collect floating plastic waste from oceans, rivers, and lakes. Features modular Aggregation Bots with AI-assisted vision, Containment Pods, and Retrieval Bots — all solar-powered. Selected from 1.5 lakh student innovations at IIT Guwahati. The project tackles 198 million tonnes of ocean garbage using intelligent, scalable solutions.

🌐 Visit swamn.com

IIT BHU Internship

Use of Radioactivity for Diagnosis of Reactor

Research internship under Prof. Rajesh Kumar Upadhyay at the Department of Chemical Engineering & Technology, IIT (BHU) Varanasi. Worked on understanding the application of radioactivity in diagnosing reactor performance and safety. Gained hands-on experience with advanced instrumentation, radiation detection sensors, and reactor diagnostics equipment.
